# HR Employee Analysis Report

## Overview

### Dataset

The dataset `HR-Excel1` comprises detailed information on 8,950 employees, with 15 key columns capturing employee demographics and professional attributes. These include identifiers such as `Employee_ID` and `Sr. No.`, personal details such as `First Name`, `Last Name`, and `Gender`, and professional information including `Education Level`, `Hiredate`, `Termdate`, `Department`, `Job Title`, `Salary`, and `Performance Rating`.

### Sample Data

The dataset represents a diverse workforce across multiple states, with a large concentration of employees in New York. It includes both male and female employees, with educational backgrounds ranging from High School through PhD. The sample also covers various job roles across departments such as Customer Service, IT, and Operations, with the displayed sample rows containing salary values ranging from $55,581 to $107,520. Performance ratings include `Needs Improvement`, `Satisfactory`, `Good`, and `Excellent`.

### Statistical Data

Key statistical insights include an average salary of $70,964.21 with a sample standard deviation of $13,763.13, indicating variation in employee salaries. The `Sr. No.` column ranges from 1 to 8,950, with a mean of 4,475.50.

The dataset provides employee demographic and professional information suitable for workforce and HR analytics.
